How it goes

To begin, I build it. I find a business with no website, or one nobody has touched in years, and build the whole thing: your hours, your menu or services, your photos, the reviews you already have. You haven't paid and you don't owe anything.

Then you get a link. I send it by text, email or in person. Open it on your phone, tell me what's wrong, and I change it until it's right.

If you like it, it goes live. I put it on your own web address, or help you get one in your name. The first month is free.

From then on, I look after it. One monthly fee covers hosting, updates and changes. New hours, a new price list, a holiday closure: text me and it's done.

Ridge is Michael, a Cal Poly grad building websites for the shops he walks past. Most have no site, or one nobody has touched in years. So I build the new one first and let the owner decide.

The name comes from the old volcanic peaks that run from Bishop Peak out to Morro Rock. You can see them from almost every street in town.

Questions

What does it cost?

It depends on the site, so there's no price list here. The site is built before you pay anything. After that it's one monthly fee for hosting, updates and changes, with no setup fee, and the first month is free.

What if I don't like it?

Then you say no, and that's the end of it. You owe nothing and the sample comes down.

I already have a website.

Most of the shops I build for do. If yours is slow, dated or hard to use on a phone, I rebuild it and keep what works, including your web address.

Do I need a web address?

If you have one, the site goes on it. If you don't, I'll help you get one, registered in your name.

How do changes work?

Text or email me what should change. New hours, prices, photos or a holiday closure usually go up within a day.

What do you need from me?

Nothing to start. If you like the sample: a few photos, a look over your hours and prices, and a yes.
