How it works

From first draft to a live site on your own web address.

I build it first.

Before you pay a cent, I build your full website: your hours, services, prices, photos and the reviews you've already earned. If you don't love it, you don't owe me anything.

Questions?

Something else on your mind? Ask me anything.

Ask me a question
What does it cost?

It depends on the site, so there's no price list here. The site is built before you pay anything. After that it's one monthly fee for hosting, updates and changes, with no setup fee.

What if I don't like it?

Then you say no, and that's the end of it. You owe nothing and the sample comes down.

I already have a website.

Most of the businesses I build for do. If yours is slow, dated or hard to use on a phone, I rebuild it and keep what works, including your web address.

Do I need a web address?

If you have one, the site goes on it. If you don't, I'll help you get one, registered in your name.

How do changes work?

Text or email me what should change. New hours, prices, photos or a holiday closure usually go up within a day.

What do you need from me?

Nothing to start. If you like the sample: a few photos, a look over your hours and prices, and a yes.

Did you build this website too?

Yep, every pixel. If you made it all the way down to the questions, I'd say it's doing its job.
